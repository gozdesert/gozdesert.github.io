01 / About

About me

I am a doctoral candidate in the Department of Statistics at Texas A&M University, advised by Professors Anirban Bhattacharya and Abhishek Chakrabortty.

I earned my master’s degree in mathematics from Pennsylvania State University and my bachelor’s degree in mathematics from Boğaziçi University.

02 / Research

Research interests

My research develops Bayesian methodologies for semiparametric inference.

I focus on robust and efficient tools for modern data problems, with applications in semi-supervised learning, causal inference, missing data, and transfer learning.
